Project assignments Which employee works on which project. Suppliers Who supplies your materials. Materials Construction materials and stock levels. Payments Payments received from clients.

Why this system

Managing information manually can lead to lost records, delays, errors in financial records, and difficulty tracking projects and materials. This system organises construction company information in a database so it is accurate and easy to find.

It helps the company keep track of project budgets, project progress, employee assignments, material stock, supplier information, and payments made by clients. Users can add, view, update and delete records in every section.

System objectives

  • Manage client information.
  • Keep employee and department records.
  • Manage construction projects and budgets.
  • Track suppliers and construction materials.
  • Record payments made for projects.
  • Manage employee project assignments.
